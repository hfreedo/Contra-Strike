# Portada de Contra Strike

Archivo final: `Contra_Strike_PC_Demo.png`.

Generada con la herramienta integrada de imágenes a partir del escenario y protagonista del CAPX y del escudo proporcionado por el docente. Dirección final solicitada: mundo alienígena fiel a los assets; personaje de espaldas, sin armas, contemplando el recorrido. Frase: «Un mundo por descubrir». La ilustración es promocional; no es una captura del juego. No se modificó el CAPX.

## Prompt final

Use case: ads-marketing. Produce the corrected art direction for a school project's PC game cover, portrait 2:3 around 1024x1536. The user's central requirement is FAITHFULNESS TO THE ACTUAL ALIEN LEVEL ASSETS and an UNARMED PROTAGONIST SEEN FROM BEHIND contemplating the world, not a cheerful generic futuristic school and not a combat poster.
Input image 1 is the game's real panoramic level. Study it closely: black/deep indigo-violet cavern background, reddish-brown ribbed/coiled alien biomechanical architecture, thin red/magenta platform tops, suspended elevated walkways, repeated glowing violet/magenta capsule-shaped fixtures, chunky conduits along ceilings and vertical supports, intricate dark patterned alien materials. Build a cinematic deep-perspective interpretation of THIS environment, preserving its colors, forms and layered side-scrolling platform identity. No sunlit atrium, tropical school, trees or bright sterile sci-fi city. Show an alien cavern that feels mysterious and full of discoveries, with purple light and some soft amber highlights. Keep it suitable for a school presentation: stylized adventure atmosphere, no horror, gore, skulls, threatening monsters or violent events.
Input image 2 is the original pixel protagonist: retain dark hair, red headband, bright blue trousers, blue/dark boots and athletic adult explorer silhouette. The protagonist stands in the LOWER LEFT / foreground on a broad red-edged platform, SEEN FROM BEHIND, full body visible, looking out toward the immense alien platforms and cavern route ahead. Back of head and back of body must face the viewer; face is not the focal point. Give him a simple plain dark sleeveless adventurer top, no ammunition belts or tactical kit. Both arms rest naturally, BOTH HANDS EMPTY and visible, correct anatomy. No rifle, pistols, knives, toy weapons, weapon holsters, combat gesture, backpack or raised fist. Calm wonder and readiness to explore, not aggression. Hero relatively small, about 30-35% image height, so the alien world is the main visual subject. The visible route winds and rises into atmospheric distance, leading the eye into the adventure.
Input image 3 is the actual school seal: preserve its circular green/gold palm-tree/book emblem and original Spanish text faithfully, as a small clean ivory medallion at bottom-right, about 14% cover width.
Typography exactly: small top corner "PC"; large beautifully readable title "CONTRA STRIKE" on two lines in upper 20%, warm metallic ivory/gold with restrained red edging; bottom subtitle "UN MUNDO POR DESCUBRIR"; small "DEMO" badge. No team names, no other copy, no awards or ratings. Premium richly painted stylized game key art, polished appealing shapes, dramatic depth and violet rim lighting. Full-bleed finished cover, no box mockup. No Counter-Strike logo or franchise branding. Retain the actual alien-level visual language rather than inventing a different setting.
